Use on your phone without internet

Checking offline files…
iPhone / iPad: Open this website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Launch the home-screen icon with an internet connection once; wait until the indicator says offline ready. You can then reopen it in Airplane Mode.
Android: Open the site in Chrome, choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu, and open the installed app online once until it says offline ready.
Notes are saved locally on this device, including when offline. Browser storage can be cleared by the system; make regular Save & backup → JSON backups. Notes do not sync between devices or between browser and home-screen installations.

Right-to-left

In auto, every line follows its own language: Persian, Arabic or Hebrew lines run right-to-left, English lines left-to-right, and empty lines keep the direction of the line above. RTL or LTR forces one direction for the whole note. Search treats Arabic and Persian ی / ک and digits as the same.
